Policy brief, 2026

Strengthening agricultural education to increase youth agripreneurship in Ghana

Written for the Ministry of Education, Ghana Education Service and the Ministry of Food and Agriculture

The brief turns the conference findings into advice for policymakers. Its central point is that better teaching alone is not enough; schools also need to change how students see agricultural careers, because about two-thirds of education's effect on intention runs through those perceptions.

  1. Add agribusiness management, entrepreneurship, digital agriculture and financial literacy to senior high school agriculture curricula.
  2. Make career exposure an explicit goal: young agripreneur role models, school–industry partnerships, farm visits and mentorship.
  3. Put rural schools first for school farms, practical facilities and laboratories, since rural students showed the stronger indirect effect.
