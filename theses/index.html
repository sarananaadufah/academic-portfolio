High school agricultural students' perceptions of agripreneurship and the future of agriculture in the Ashanti Region of Ghana

MSc Agricultural Economics, Akdeniz University, Türkiye · 2024–2026 · Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Yavuz Taşcıoğlu

Ghana needs young people to replace an ageing farming population, yet few agricultural science graduates move into agribusiness. This study asked whether students who see a positive future for agriculture are more likely to plan an agricultural enterprise, and what holds them back. It used a convergent mixed-methods design: a stratified survey of 274 students in eight public senior high schools across eight districts, with four 5-point Likert scales (Cronbach's α = 0.937–0.984; KMO = 0.964–0.977) and seven open-ended questions.

  • Perception of agriculture (OR = 13.84) and entrepreneurial traits (OR = 11.22) both predicted high intention in Firth-corrected logistic regression; gender, residence and parents' education did not.
  • Students named economic incentives (23.4%) and government support (14.6%) as their main motivators, and limited practical experience (16.2%) and outdated curriculum content (15.1%) as further barriers.
  • Analysed in SPSS and R (correlation, t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square, multiple linear regression), with thematic and SWOT coding of the open-ended answers.

Financial viability of aquaponics systems in the Ashanti Region

BSc Agribusiness Management, KNUST, Ghana · 2021 · Group dissertation (five students) · Supervisor: Dr. Gifty Boakye Appiah

Aquaponics produces fish and vegetables from the same water, which suits peri-urban farmers with little land. Five-year cash flows for catfish and tilapia systems were built in Excel from interviews with 12 aquaponics farmers reached by snowball sampling, and production constraints were ranked with Kendall's coefficient of concordance.

Both systems were viable, and tilapia paid back faster (5.40 against 6.58 months). High feed cost and power fluctuation were the two most pressing constraints (p < 0.01), which points to feed and energy costs as the first targets for support.
