Mapping project

Aquaponics site suitability

Ashanti Region, Ghana · Follows on from the BSc study

Click the map to enlarge.Open the full-size map

To guide where feed subsidies and starter kits for aquaponics should go, I used vector buffers to mark land near rivers and water bodies and then combined it with markets and settlements through attribute joins and a multi-criteria overlay. Suitable zones follow river corridors and cluster around Kumasi.
