Mapping project

Maize yield gaps between districts

Northern Region, Ghana

Click the map to enlarge.Open the full-size map

To show where extension services and rural credit should be prioritised, I used table joins to link district maize yields to boundaries and choropleth mapping to compare districts. Yields ranged from 1.2 to 3.63 t/ha; Karaga, Saboba and both Nanumba districts fell in the lowest class and Kpandai in the highest.
