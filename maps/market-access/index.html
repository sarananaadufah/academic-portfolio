Mapping project

Market access and smallholder vulnerability

Ashanti Region, Ghana

Map available on request.

District-level data hides large differences in market access within a district, so I used network analysis to model the two-hour travel reach of each market hub from road speeds and convex hulls to map each catchment. The analysis shows the smallholder communities that fall outside every catchment.
