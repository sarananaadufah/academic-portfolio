Mapping project

Climate stress zones for maize

Ashanti Region, Ghana

Click the map to enlarge.Open the full-size map

To locate where heat- and drought-tolerant maize varieties are most needed, I used the Raster Calculator to combine March temperature and rainfall rasters and reclassification to flag land at or above 34 °C with 90 mm of rain or less. Most of this high-stress land lies in the north-eastern districts.
