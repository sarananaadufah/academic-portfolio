Mapping project

Drought stress from Sentinel-2 imagery

Western Brandenburg, Germany · July 2022

Click the map to enlarge.Open the full-size map

To show how satellite data can point field teams to stressed cropland before a site visit, I calculated NDVI from Sentinel-2 bands for the agricultural plain during the 2022 summer drought, masked the scene and grouped the values into five classes from bare soil to dense vegetation. High stress is concentrated in the north-west of the scene.
